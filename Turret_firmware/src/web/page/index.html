Status

Sleep

…
Radar sleep: the radar keeps watching, everything else rests; a target, the knob, PROG or Wake up wakes the turret. Deep sleep (v2 board): almost nothing draws current; the turret wakes up when moved, on PROG, at the end of the night or for a patrol, and this page is unreachable until then. Delays, night, WiFi and patrols: Settings tab, group Sleep.
